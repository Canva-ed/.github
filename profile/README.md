## Why Canva for Mac?

The Mac ecosystem is defined by thoughtful design, precision, and performance. Canva for Mac honours that tradition. This isn't merely a port of the browser experience; it's a reimagination of how a design tool should behave on Apple hardware.

- **Native performance** — Leverages macOS frameworks for smooth rendering and efficient resource usage.
- **Offline capability** — Continue designing without an active internet connection.
- **System integration** — Works seamlessly with macOS features such as Dark Mode, Trackpad gestures, and native file pickers.
- **Keyboard-first workflow** — Full shortcut support designed for Mac users who value speed.

Canva for Mac is for designers, marketers, educators, and anyone who needs to communicate visually without spending hours mastering complex toolbars.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Canva for Mac free?**  
Canva for Mac is free to download and use with a Canva Free account. Premium features are available with Canva Pro or Canva for Teams subscriptions.

**Does it work offline?**  
Yes. Designs you've opened or created remain accessible offline. Changes are automatically synced once you reconnect.

**Can I use my existing Canva web content?**  
Absolutely. Your designs, folders, and brand kits are synchronised across all platforms.

**Is collaboration supported?**  
Yes. You can share designs, invite collaborators, comment, and co-edit in real time.

**Is Canva for Mac secure for enterprise use?**  
Yes. Canva complies with industry-standard security protocols including SOC 2, GDPR, and encryption at rest and in transit.
